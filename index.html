COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know before we begin.

Here are a few details that help make the first conversation easier.

What do I need to share for a data analysis project?

Usually, a spreadsheet or CSV file and a short explanation of the business questions you want answered. Do not send passwords or sensitive customer information.

Can I request only one service?

Yes. You can enquire about a single sales analysis, dashboard, data-cleaning task, business card or promotional design.

How are project prices decided?

Pricing depends on the scope, complexity, format and timeline. You can request a quote before deciding whether to proceed.

How will I receive the finished work?

The delivery format is agreed at the start. Depending on the project, it may include a report, cleaned file, dashboard or design files.
